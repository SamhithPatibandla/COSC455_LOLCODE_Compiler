   |             ---- -------- ^^^^ second mutable borrow occurs here"

ASSISTANT [excerpt]:
"These are Rust borrowing errors (E0499). Both errors come from trying to call two methods that require mutable access to `self` in the same expression.

The fix is simple: store the character in a variable first, then pass it to `add_char()`."

Suggested correction:
    let c = self.get_char();
    self.add_char(c);
